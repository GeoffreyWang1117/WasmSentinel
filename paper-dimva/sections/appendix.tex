\section{Extended Experimental Results}
\label{app:experiments}

This appendix provides additional data from our systematic evaluation of \textsc{WasmSentinel}.

\subsection{MLP Hyperparameter Optimization}
We evaluated various MLP architectures to find the optimal balance between detection accuracy and inference latency. As shown in Table~\ref{tab:mlp-sweep}, a 4-layer architecture with 128-64-32-16 hidden units provides the best trade-off.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{MLP architecture sweep results.}
\label{tab:mlp-sweep}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
\textbf{Architecture} & \textbf{F1 Score} & \textbf{Latency ($\mu$s)} \\
\midrule
2-layer (64-32) & 0.76 & 0.08 \\
3-layer (64-32-16) & 0.81 & 0.12 \\
\textbf{4-layer (128-64-32-16)} & \textbf{0.84} & \textbf{0.20} \\
5-layer (128-128-64-32-16) & 0.84 & 0.35 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Threshold Analysis}
The escalation threshold $\theta_1$ in our hybrid architecture determines the percentage of events passed to the MLP. Figure~\ref{fig:threshold-analysis} (data omitted for space) shows that $\theta_1 = 0.3$ minimizes overhead while maintaining 100\% of the MLP's baseline recall.

\section{Feature Space Definition}
\label{app:features}
The input feature vector (340 dimensions) consists of:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Syscall Frequencies (100):} Normalized counts of common system calls.
    \item \textbf{N-gram Hashes (100):} Count of observed 3-gram patterns.
    \item \textbf{Statistical Features (40):} Mean, variance, and entropy of syscall numbers.
    \item \textbf{Dangerous Indicators (100):} Indicators for sensitive file access, network connections, and privilege escalation.
\end{itemize}
All features are scaled to $[0,1]$ before being fed into the MLP.
